05 — The Takeaway
⚡

Not syllables — statistics

BPE is purely frequency-driven, not linguistic. It's language-agnostic — working across English, code, emoji, and any language. Common patterns compress into single tokens; rare or technical jargon costs more. This means a highly technical prompt consumes more context window than a casual one of the same word count.
